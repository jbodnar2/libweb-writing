## Visit the Library

Welcome to the Georgia State University Library. Current GSU students, faculty, and staff; visitors, and alumni are welcome to use the library during regular hours. See the [Library Hours](https://library.gsu.edu/about/library-hours/) for exact times and holidays.

### Entry Requirements

All patrons must show a valid ID at the security desk. We currently do not accept digital IDs.

- **GSU students, faculty, and staff**: Valid PantherCard.
- **Visitors and alumni**: Government-issued photo ID (no photocopies or images of IDs).
- **Guests under 16**: Must be accompanied by an adult.

### Library Access for Visitors, Alumni & Retirees

The following options are available to those who are **not currently enrolled or employed** by Georgia State University.

#### General Access

- **Government-issued photo ID required**.
- We recommend contacting an [archivist](https://library.gsu.edu/special-collections/), [subject librarian](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/), or [research data specialist](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/data-services/) at least **one week before your visit** to confirm arrangements.

#### Accessing Online Databases

Visitors have two options:

1.  **Request a** **[Guest or Affiliate Network Account](https://technology.gsu.edu/technology-services/accounts-passwords-campusid/)** sponsored by a university unit, or
2.  Connect to the ”**Visitor@GSU**” **ClearPass Wi-Fi**, obtain **OpenAthens credentials** at the Service Desk, and login at [https://my.openathens.net](https://my.openathens.net) after selecting “Georgia State University”.

#### Borrowing Privileges

- **Emeriti faculty**: Retain full library privileges through automatic [Emeriti Association](https://emeriti.gsu.edu/) membership.
- **[Community Borrower’s Card](https://secure.touchnet.com/C20797_ustores/web/product_detail.jsp?PRODUCTID=2589&SINGLESTORE=true)**: $30.00 (5 books, 4-week loan, 2 renewals). Email [libdev@gsu.edu](mailto:libdev@gsu.edu) or [404-413-2700](tel:404-413-2700) for assistance.
- **Visiting faculty**: May request **academic department sponsorship** for borrowing privileges. Contact the sponsoring department for details.

#### Additional Services

- **Interlibrary Loan (ILL)** and **GIL Express** are available to visitors with a [Guest or Affiliate Network Account](https://technology.gsu.edu/technology-services/accounts-passwords-campusid/) and a **CampusID** sponsored by a university unit.
