# Research Tools

## [Research Tools](https://library.gsu.edu/research/research-tools/)

These are the resources you need to find books, articles, primary sources, Georgia State theses and dissertations and more.

#### [Library Catalog](https://galileo-usg-gsu-primo.hosted.exlibrisgroup.com/primo-explore/search?vid=GSU_V1&tab=default_tab&query=any,contains,&search_scope=GSU&sortby=rank&lang=en_US)

Search the library catalog to find books, DVDs, music scores, journals and more.

#### [Databases](http://research.library.gsu.edu/az.php)

Databases help you find articles published in scholarly journals, magazines and newspapers.

#### [GALILEO](http://www.galileo.usg.edu/scholar/gsu/search/)

Library resources provided to Georgia citizens by the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia.

NOTE: GALILEO-sponsored resources are listed within the database directory, linked above.

#### [ScholarWorks](http://scholarworks.gsu.edu/)

Highlights the research and scholarship of members of the university community, including theses and dissertations.

#### [Digital Collections](http://digitalcollections.library.gsu.edu/cdm/)

Unique digitized resources from the Library’s Special Collections and Archives Department.

#### [Exhibits](https://exhibits.library.gsu.edu)

Collaborative digital projects that highlight Library resources.

#### [ArchivesSpace](https://archivesspace.library.gsu.edu/)

Search and find detailed information about archival collections in Special Collections and Archives’ finding-aid database.
