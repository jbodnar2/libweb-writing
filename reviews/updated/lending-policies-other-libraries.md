**Request Methods Accepted:**  
We prefer that requests be submitted via OCLC (WorldCat Resource Sharing/ILLiad). If you do not have access to OCLC, please use our [online web form](https://gsu.illiad.oclc.org/illiad/lending/lendinglogon.html). First time users, please contact us by [email](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/interlibrary-loan/contact-interlibrary-loan/) to obtain a username and password. Please include a contact person’s name,  email address, and shipping/billing address. We no longer accept requests via fax, email, Ariel, or phone.

**Fees and Billing Policies:**  
We supply material free to ARCHE, USG, GOLD, and ASERL libraries. We do not charge SOLINE libraries for loans, or for articles if you do not charge us for articles.

**For U.S. and Canada academic, public, and government libraries other than the consortial partners listed above:**

- Loans: $15 per item, 1-4 volumes/reels or 1-20 fiche per bibliographic record.
- Photocopies: $15 per article for articles 1-30 pages, additional .25 per page for 31-50 pages.
- Invoice Fee: $10 additional for invoice sent with item or to follow by mail. We welcome payment by IFM instead, and also accept IFLA vouchers. We do not accept credit card payments.
- Rush Fee: $10 additional

**For U.S. and Canada corporate/for profit libraries:**

- Loans: $30 per item, 1-4 volumes/reels or 1-20 fiche per bibliographic record.
- Photocopies: $30 for 1-30 pages, additional .25 per page for 31-50 pages.
- Invoice Fee: $15 additional for invoice sent with item or to follow by mail. We welcome payment by IFM instead, and also accept IFLA vouchers. We do not accept credit card payments.
- Rush service is not supported.

**For International Libraries (excluding Canada):**

- Loans: $30 per item, 1-4 volumes/reels or 1-20 fiche per bibliographic record.
- Photocopies: $30 for 1-30 pages, additional .25 per page for 31-50 pages.
- Invoice Fee: $15 additional for invoice sent with item or to follow by mail. We welcome payment by IFM instead, and also accept IFLA vouchers. We do not accept credit card payments.
- Rush Fee: $10 additional

**Loan Length:**  
Our standard loan period for returnables is 10 weeks with no renewal.

**Recalls:**  
All loans are subject to immediate recall if requested by one of our own patrons.

**Delivery Methods:**  
We ship loans via 1st class mail to Alaska and Hawaii, and to academic, public, and government libraries paying rush fees. We ship via international airmail outside the United States. All other loans are shipped library rate unless you supply your UPS account number. We supply articles via Odyssey, OCLC Article Exchange, 1st class mail, or international airmail. We do not supply articles as email attachments.

**Material which does not circulate:**  
Periodicals, audio-visual material, reference material, maps, computer files. We consider requests for VHS format on a case by case basis for ARCHE and USG libraries only. Copy and loan requests from our Special Collections and Archives are considered on a case by case basis.

**Georgia State theses and dissertations:**  
Most Georgia State theses and dissertations submitted since December 2004 are available in electronic format in the [ScholarWorks @ Georgia State University](http://scholarworks.gsu.edu/). University Library has two copies of most theses and dissertations submitted before December 2004: a circulating copy and a copy held in our Special Collections and Archives. We lend print format dissertations and theses through Interlibrary Loan on a case-by-case basis.
