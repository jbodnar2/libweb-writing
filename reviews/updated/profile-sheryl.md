Biography

Sheryl Williams is the head of the Interlibrary Loan unit, which borrows books and obtains articles not available at the University Library when needed by our patrons. The unit also supplies materials to other libraries, and scans articles from the University Library print collection for electronic delivery to Georgia State University faculty, staff, and students.
