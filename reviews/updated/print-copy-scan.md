[Printing](#1560361943439-e0f6761b-0f51)

Printing is available at all Library locations and is provided through the [PantherPrint](https://technology.gsu.edu/technology-services/it-services/labs-and-classrooms/computer-labs/pantherprint/) service. A PantherCard or [PantherCash Guest Card](https://services.gsu.edu/panthercard/#5) is required for printing.

#### How do I print?

Follow the steps provided by Technology Services: [How to Print On Campus with PantherPrint](https://gsutech.service-now.com/sp?id=kb_article&sys_id=274ed843db541740186e5058dc9619c4)

#### How do I pay for printing?

A printing credit is added to student PantherCards at the start of every semester.

After all print credit is used, additional printing is charged to your [PantherCash](https://panthercard.gsu.edu/panthercash/) account. [Registration](https://cdn.gsu.edu/pantherCash/#/login) is required and additional deposits can be made [online](https://gsupanthercard-sp.transactcampus.com/eAccounts/AnonymousHome.aspx) or in person at the PantherCard Office.

[PantherCash Guest Cards](https://services.gsu.edu/panthercard/#5) are available for $1 from any Value Transfer Station (VTS). Additional funds are required for printing. Exact cash deposits can be made at VTS machines.

#### I used up all my printing money. What can I do?

After all print credit is used, additional printing is charged to your [PantherCash](https://panthercard.gsu.edu/panthercash/) account. [Registration](https://cdn.gsu.edu/pantherCash/#/login) is required and additional deposits can be made [online](https://gsupanthercard-sp.transactcampus.com/eAccounts/AnonymousHome.aspx) or in person at the PantherCard Office.

[PantherCash Guest Cards](https://services.gsu.edu/panthercard/#5) are available for $1 from any Value Transfer Station (VTS). Additional funds are required for printing. Exact cash deposits can be made at VTS machines.

#### Can I print from my laptop/tablet/mobile device?

Yes. Follow the instructions provided by Technology Services to print from your personal device: [How to Print Using MobilePrint](https://gsutech.service-now.com/sp?id=kb_article&sys_id=ee70244bdb541740186e5058dc96197f)
