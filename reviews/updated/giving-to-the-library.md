### Support the Library

The Georgia State University Library is a hub for research, learning, and community engagement. From curating special collections to providing cutting-edge technology and study spaces, we strive to meet the evolving needs of our users.

Help us continue this success by making a financial contribution to the Georgia State University Library.

### Your Impact

Your generosity helps us:

- **Provide More Resources** – Expand our collections and provide cutting-edge digital resources.
- **Transform Spaces** – Create collaborative and innovative learning environments.
- **Offer Engaging Events and Activities** –Support special initiatives, such as exhibits, lectures, and programming that enrich the university and local community.
- **Preserve History** – Showcase materials that highlight diverse voices and stories of Georgia State and the greater Atlanta community.

### Ways to Give

**Support an Existing Fund:** Funds managed by the Georgia State Foundation support collection building, student success, preservation, programming, scholarly activities and other aspects of the University Library’s work. To learn more about individual funds and the goals they support, visit [Georgia State University Library Funds](https://library.gsu.edu/giving/library-funds/).

**Planned Giving:** Consider including the University Library in your will or estate plan. A deferred gift can enable you to make a significant contribution with profound impact, provide income for your family, reduce taxes and establish a lasting legacy for you and your family at the University Library.

**Endowments:** Endowments provide endless support to the University Library and enable us to fulfill our mission in perpetuity. We encourage you to contribute to one of our existing endowed funds or make an investment in the library’s future by establishing a new fund. The minimum amount required to establish an endowment is $25,000.

**Corporate/Foundation Gifts:** The Georgia State Office of Corporate and Foundation Relations (CFR) facilitates mutually beneficial collaborations, partnerships, and sponsorships in areas such as philanthropy, sponsored research, student recruitment, technology transfer, and continuing education. Contact CFR to discuss making a corporate or foundation gift to the University Library.

**For more information about planned gifts, endowments or other financial gifts, contact [University Advancement](https://giving.gsu.edu/contact/ "Original URL: https://giving.gsu.edu/contact/. Click or tap if you trust this link.").**

### Other Ways to Help

**The Georgia State University Libraries can no longer accept donations of printed books, journals, and other materials for the general collection**. Please consult our [Materials Donation Policy](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/zvunovzbj8yuby0tb6qdf/materials-donation-policy.pdf?rlkey=542tyd5b3d8w954ll2hrrwvc1&st=u68zsjoq&dl=0) for additional information as well as recommendations of other organizations that will accept printed materials.

**We welcome gifts of books written by Georgia State faculty that are not currently in our general collection**. Please contact [Skye Hardesty, skye@gsu.edu](https://library.gsu.edu/giving/skye@gsu.edu), Head, Collection Development & Discovery, to donate this material.

Would you like to donate historical materials for future researchers? Visit our [Donating to Special Collections and Archives](https://library.gsu.edu/special-collections/donate/) page to learn more about donating to our rare and unique collections.
