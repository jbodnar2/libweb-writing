[Students](#1559236345989-a9fb4470-68f9)

A valid PantherCard is required for checking out materials.

See the [Equipment Borrowing](/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/borrow-equipment/) page for details about checking out laptops, cameras, and other equipment.

[Faculty](#1559236346022-50321f15-ecc9)

A valid PantherCard is required for checking out materials.

Faculty may submit a [Proxy Authorization form](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/proxy-authorization-request/) to allow another user to check out library materials on their behalf.

Part-time and adjunct instructors have the same privileges as graduate students.

[Alumni & Other](#1559236555034-c8e63156-c772)

#### Visiting Scholars

- The sponsoring department or program must submit a written request on behalf of the visiting scholar requesting borrowing privileges. The request should include the Campus ID and Panther ID Number of the visiting scholar, the length of time of sponsorship, and description of research needs. Email requests to libcirc@gsu.edu. See the [How can the library support visiting scholars](https://answers.library.gsu.edu/faq/371696) page for a summary of services available.

#### Alumni & Community

- Alumni and members of the community may [purchase a Community Borrower Card for $30](https://secure.touchnet.com/C20797_ustores/web/product_detail.jsp?PRODUCTID=2589&SINGLESTORE=true). The card is valid for 365 days from the purchase date.
- Check out up to 5 books from the General Collection for 4 weeks with 2 renewals.
- Overdue items are charged $0.25 per day.
- These same privileges are also a benefit of Lifetime membership in the Georgia State University Alumni Association. Lifetime Alumni Association members may contact the University Library Administrative Office by emailing [libdev@gsu.edu](mailto:libdev@gsu.edu) or calling 404-413-2700.

#### ARCHE Students, Faculty & Staff

- ARCHE users are required to show a current [ARCHE Borrower Card](https://www.atlantahighered.org/collaboration/library-collaboration/interlibrary-loan-program/interlibrary-use-policies-procedures/) issued from their home institution.
- Check out up to 5 books from the General Collection for 4 weeks with 2 renewals.
- Overdue items are charged $0.25 per day.

#### Emory University Students, Faculty & Staff

- Emory users are required to show a current Emory ID.
- Check out up to 5 books from the General Collection for 4 weeks with 2 renewals.
- Overdue items are charged $0.25 per day.

#### University System of Georgia Students, Faculty & Staff

- University System of Georgia users are required to show a current campus ID or a government-issued photo ID to check out materials in person.
- Check out books from the General Collection for 90 days.

#### Board of Regents Administrative Staff

- Board of Regents Administrative Staff are required to show a current Board of Regents ID card.
- Same library privileges as Georgia State faculty.

#### State Legislators

Same library privileges as Georgia State students.

#### Other Visitors

Please inquire at a library service desk or administrative office.
