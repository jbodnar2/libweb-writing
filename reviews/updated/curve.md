# CURVE

## [CURVE](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/curve/)

Located on Atlanta, Library South 2, the Library’s Collaborative University Research and Visualization Environment (CURVE) is a technology-rich discovery space supporting the research and digital scholarship of students, faculty, and staff.

The CURVE’s mission is to enhance research and visualizations by providing technology and spaces that promote interdisciplinary engagement, collaborative investigation and innovative inquiry.

### interactWall

The CURVE’s centerpiece technology, the interactWall, is a large-scale video wall that is flexible and easy to use—yet powerful enough to render complex models and visualizations. The wall itself measures 24 by 4.5 feet, with more than 25 million active pixels.

![Students using the interactWall to pan and scale maps details](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2019/06/curve1.jpg)

For those wishing to use PowerPoint on the interactWall, download the [PowerPoint template](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/i2jreiqh8f3aulkyypppj/CURVEinteractWall-PPT-Template-1r1ks2l.pptx?rlkey=4slfd112f3dkds4ht9ta7gbpc&st=ybwwivwd&dl=0) so your content will expand across the entire display.

### Collaborative Research Workstations

These workstations support advanced research, digital scholarship and original creative activities for small groups or individuals.

### **Consultations**

Specialists are available by appointment to provide consultations in CURVE in the following areas:

- **Library Research**
  - Finding Articles, Books, & More
  - Citation Management
  - Systematic & Scoping Reviews
  - Contact your [Subject Librarian](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/) to learn more and schedule an appointment.

- **Research Computing (ARCTIC)**
  - Support for Advanced Scientific Computing, AI, & Data Science
  - High Performance Computing, Storage, & Networking for Research and Teaching
  - Email the [ARCTIC Team](https://arctic.gsu.edu/about-arctic/our-team/) at [arc@gsu.edu](mailto:arc@gsu.edu) to learn more and schedule an appointment.

- **Research Data Services**
  - Training on Data Analysis & Visualization Tools & Methods
  - Finding Data & Statistics Resources
  - Email the [Research Data Services Team](https://research.library.gsu.edu/rds) at [libdatahelp@gsu.edu](mailto:libdatahelp@gsu.edu) to learn more and schedule an appointment.
