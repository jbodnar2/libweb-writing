# Borrow Equipment

## [Borrow Equipment](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/borrow-equipment/)

You may checkout the [equipment listed below](#1559249751117-006fc24d-c708) on a first-come, first-serve basis or [reserve in advance](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/equipment/atlanta-equipment). You must return equipment to the library you borrowed them from.

- At the Atlanta campus library, checkout first-come, first-serve equipment at the service desk, Library North 1 and pickup reserved equipment at the [Panther Pickup Lockers](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/panther-pickup-lockers/)
- At a Perimeter campus library, checkout first-come, first-serve equipment at the service desk

Read about [our services for patrons with disabilities](https://library.gsu.edu/about/accessibility/) for information about accessibility equipment.

Students, faculty, and staff can find [additional equipment for checkout](https://technology.gsu.edu/technology-services/software-equipment/equipment-checkout/) from a CATLab.

[Equipment Borrowing Policies](#1559249751135-4c8e5472-8bb3)

- Only active students, faculty and staff can borrow equipment, with preference given to students.
- A PantherCard or a government-issued photo ID is required to borrow equipment.
- Only one of each item type can be borrowed at the same time.
- Loan periods vary. Overdue fines for first-come, first-serve equipment is $5 per day for items with one-week loan periods and $5 per hour for items with an eight-hour loan period. Overdue fines for reservable equipment are found in the item details description.
- Borrowing polices for books and media items can be found on the [Borrowing Policies](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/borrowing-policies/) page.
