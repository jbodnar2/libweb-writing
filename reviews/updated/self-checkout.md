# Self Checkout

## [Self Checkout](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/self-checkout/)

Self Checkout is a fast, easy way to borrow books using your own device or from a kiosk near a service desk. Each kiosk includes a security station to desensitize books before they leave the library.

### Kiosk

You can check out books using a self checkout kiosk near the service desk at Atlanta, Clarkston, Decatur, Dunwoody, or Newton.

### Mobile

You are required to be inside a Georgia State library to download the Georgia State Self Checkout app to your device. Allow the app to access your camera to scan barcodes and your location so the app knows which library you are in.

Search for Georgia State Self Checkout to download the app for your [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ca.bintec.meescan.c84053978) or [Apple](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/georgia-state-self-checkout/id1535102134) device.
