# Working Together

## [Working Together](https://library.gsu.edu/working-together/)

Your University Library is your partner in success. Our people, spaces, and resources can help you thrive, not just survive, in college. We are here to support all your academic needs.

From technology equipment and study spaces to friendly faces eager to assist you with research, we’re with you every step of the way.

You can succeed. We can help you. Let’s work together.

### Save Money by Borrowing Calculators and Other Equipment

![Calculator](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2024/08/calculator-300x200.png)

Calculators, cameras, headphones, and other items ready to borrow.

Need a calculator, Chromebook, or headphones? Your library has you covered! At your campus library service desk, you can borrow all kinds of equipment to complete projects.

Learn about the [equipment you can borrow](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/borrow-equipment/) to get your work done.

### Find Spaces to Collaborate

![Students talking in the library](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2024/08/2-female-students-300x200.png)

Need a place to gather and get work done? From group projects to group study sessions, your library provides flexible spaces for collaboration and teamwork. Learn more at your library’s service desk.

[Learn about collaborative spaces](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/) (and other places to get work done at the library).

### Find a Space to Focus on Work

![Pounce asking for silence](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2024/08/quiet-pounce-300x200.png)

Silent study areas offer distraction-free study spaces.

Need a place to study and focus on work? Your library provides safe and flexible spaces where you can block out distractions. Ask a staff member where you can find a space that works for you.

### Turn Your Creative Vision to Reality

![Recording a podcast in the recording studio.](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2024/08/podcasting-300x200.png)

Recording studios are your creative playground.

Want to record a podcast or produce a video? The Atlanta campus library has multimedia rooms with the software and equipment you need to turn your creative vision into reality. Stop by the Library North first floor service desk for more information.

[Learn about multimedia rooms and other spaces](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/).

### Experts Are Here to Help You

![Researcher in special collections](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2024/08/researching-300x200.png)

Experts can guide you to the best materials for your project.

Not sure how to find quality research materials for a project or paper? Librarians, archivists and data services specialists are ready to connect you with the resources and information you need to succeed. Stop by your campus library service desk to learn more.

[Find your librarian](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/) here.
