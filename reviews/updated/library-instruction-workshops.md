# Library Instruction & Workshops

## [Library Instruction & Workshops](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/library-instruction-and-workshops/)

The Library helps improve students’ research competency through a variety of instructional services.

### Library Instruction Sessions

Our instruction sessions give students practice with library research. We offer one-time and multiple sessions to develop student research skills.

Instruction sessions can be held in person or online. Librarians will work with you to determine the best options for your class, synchronous or asynchronous.

#### How to Schedule a Library Instruction Session

Scheduling a library instruction session differs for Atlanta and Perimeter faculty.

**Atlanta faculty**, please [contact your department’s subject librarian](/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/) to arrange for a library instruction session.

**Perimeter faculty**, please use our [instruction request form (Perimeter)](/services-and-spaces/research-services/library-instruction-and-workshops/instruction-request-form/) to arrange a library instruction session.

### Workshops & Video Tutorials

We offer a variety of online and face-to-face workshops throughout the year on topics such as scholarly impact, use of library databases, data management, and software applications.

See our [Library Workshops and Events calendar](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/calendar/workshops/?cid=2352&t=d&d=0000-00-00&cal=2352) for a workshop that interests you.

We also maintain [video tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWGUpLkOG-NZH-N6A2jcgSA) for help searching library databases, understanding and installing Zotero, and more.

### Research Guides

Research guides are doorways to the research process. They help students identify and use resources effectively. They can stand alone or go with a research instruction session.

Review our [list of research guides](https://research.library.gsu.edu) for one that suits your needs or interests.

#### How to Request a New Research Guide

Don’t see a research guide that suits your needs? Your librarian can create a guide for any (or all) of your iCollege courses.

Please [contact your subject librarian or campus librarian](/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/) with questions about requesting a new research guide.
