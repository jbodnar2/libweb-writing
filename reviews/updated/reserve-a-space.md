# Reserve a Space

## [Reserve a Space](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/reserve-a-space/)

Active Georgia State University students, faculty, and staff can use the library for:

- Studying and research related to coursework
- Research in their field of study
- Duties related to Georgia State University employment

We only allow filming and photography by special permission and in support of university coursework or administrative activities. Requests must be submitted 1 week in advance of project start date: [Filming & Photography Request Form](https://library.gsu.edu/about/policies/film-request-form/)

### Group Study Rooms

Groups of 2 or more may reserve study rooms up to 21 days in advance for group study, projects, and research. All study rooms have a whiteboard, and some may have TV displays. Dry erase markers are available for checkout from a service desk (PantherCard required.) Please see our [Group Study Room Policy](https://answers.library.gsu.edu/policies/faq/433943) for more information.

**Atlanta** library study rooms are unlocked, and reservations take priority over walk-ins.

**Perimeter** library study rooms are locked. Keys are available for checkout from a service desk (PantherCard required.) At least 2 people must be present within the first 15 minutes. Reservations are forfeited after 15 minutes.

- [Reserve an Atlanta study room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/atlanta)
- No study rooms at Alpharetta
- [Reserve a Clarkston study room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/clarkston)
- [Reserve a Decatur study room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/decatur)
- [Reserve a Dunwoody study room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/dunwoody)
- [Reserve a Newton study room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/newton)

### Media, Gaming Spaces

Located on Atlanta Library North 2 and available for small groups or individuals. Reservations are required for access. Check in at the service desk, Library North 1.

- [Reserve a Media or Gaming Space](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/tech-spaces)
- Videoconferencing Studio [Quick Guide](https://research.library.gsu.edu/ld.php?content_id=73037139) (pdf) and [Equipment and Software](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/space/37433)
- Recording Studios [Quick Guide](https://research.library.gsu.edu/ld.php?content_id=73037012) (pdf) and [Equipment and Software](https://research.library.gsu.edu/recordingstudios)
- Gaming & VR Lab [Equipment and Games](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/space/37439)

### Computer Spaces

Small groups and individuals may reserve Bloomberg Terminals, located in the Study Commons and on Atlanta Library North 1.

- [Reserve the Study Commons Bloomberg Terminal](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/bloombergsc/bloomberg)
- [Reserve the 1st Floor Bloomberg Terminal](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/bloomberg/bloomberg)
- Bloomberg [Guide](https://research.library.gsu.edu/bloomberg)

### Individual Spaces

Individuals may reserve a Mindfulness Room, located on Atlanta Library North 2, or the Lactation Wellness Room, located on Atlanta Study Commons. Reservations required for access.

- [Reserve a Mindfulness Room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/mindfulness)
- [Reserve the Lactation Wellness Room](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/reserve/spaces/private)

### Exhibit, Display, Event Spaces

The library has spaces for occasional events sponsored by GSU departments. See our [Policies](https://library.gsu.edu/about/policies/) page for more information about the Library North Rooftop Terrace, Library Exhibit Gallery, Colloquium Room, and spaces for tabling to interact with students and other library users.
