# Research Visits

## [Research Visits](https://library.gsu.edu/special-collections/research-visits/)

### Before You Visit

1.  **Create an account in [Aeon](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon) to schedule an appointment.**
    - GSU users: Click **GSU Users (Panther Card)** and then **First Time Users** (bottom of the next screen). Sign in using your GSU credentials and follow the prompts.
    - Non-GSU users: Click **Non-GSU Users** and then **First Time Users** (bottom of the next screen), then **First Time Users Click Here** and follow the prompts. Once logged in, you can schedule appointments by clicking **Appointments** on the menu across the top of the screen.
2.  **After creating your account, [sign into Aeon](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon) and select Appointments to schedule a visit.**
3.  **Please request materials in advance (strongly recommended):**
    - Items stored off-site require **at least three (3) business days** for retrieval.
    - Weather conditions, holidays, weekends, and staff availability may delay retrieval.
    - We **cannot guarantee** access to material requested during your visit.
4.  **How to request items in [ArchivesSpace](https://archivesspace.library.gsu.edu/):**
    - Navigate to a specific item or folder and click **Request this item** (top right).
    - At the collection level, ArchivesSpace displays “Not requestable.”
    - Selecting a single item or folder will create a request for the entire box to be pulled for your appointment.
    - You will be directed to complete your request in Aeon.
    - Items that display “Access restricted” cannot be requested.
5.  **Finding online resources:**
    - Many items are already digitized and **available online** in our [Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.library.gsu.edu/).
    - [Research guides](https://research.library.gsu.edu/archivalresearch) for working in the archives are also available.

### Visiting the Library

- **To enter the building** bring your government-issued ID or PantherCard.
- **Location:** Special Collections & Archives, Library South, 8th floor.
- **Plan your visit:**
  - [Visit the Library](https://library.gsu.edu/about/visit-the-library/)
  - [Getting to Campus](https://parking.gsu.edu/getting-to-campus/#atlanta-campus)
  - [Visitor Parking](https://parking.gsu.edu/parking/visitors-parking/)
- **Direct questions and accommodation requests to** [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu)

### When You Arrive at the Reading Room

1.  **Ring the doorbell on the Reading Room door.** We will greet and admit you. A staff or faculty member must be present in the Reading Room for all research appointments.
2.  **Show your ID,** PantherCard or government issued ID.
3.  **Store personal items in a locker,** including all coats, umbrellas, and other outerwear.
4.  **Only a laptop, cellphone, and camera are allowed on your table.** (Pencil and paper are provided.)
5.  **Prohibited at the research stations:** No food, drinks (liquid), gum, or pens.
6.  **No Firearms:** The Reading Room is an administrative space—no firearms, concealed or otherwise, are permitted.

### Handling Materials

- **Physical materials may be used only in the Reading Room** and only during regular hours.
- **Tell staff which box, book, or object you would like to see first.** We will bring the item to your table.
- **At your table, only one box, book, or object is permitted at a time.** Groups must also follow this rule.
- **Remove one folder at a time.** (On request, staff can provide an “out card” placeholder).
- **Maintain the original order:** Turn pages like a book—do not shuffle contents.
- **Protect our collections:** Do not lean on, write on, or trace over papers. Avoid handling books and periodicals in a way that may damage the spine.
- **If an item seems fragile or out of order,** ask for staff assistance.

### Photography & Duplication

- **You may photograph or copy documents** that you are unable to review in the Reading Room.
- **All images must include our property rights statement slip.**
- **For publication use** (in a book, website, article, documentary, for example), speak with us first. Licensing and reproduction fees may apply. We do not hold copyright for all collections and may not be able to grant permission to use items.
- **If you share items on social media,** please tag our accounts and follow fair use guidelines.
- **We offer limited [duplication services](https://library.gsu.edu/special-collections/services/#duplication)** (small sections of a folder or items). We cannot duplicate entire folders, boxes, or collections. Fees may apply.
- **Review our [camera use policy](https://library.gsu.edu/wp-content/blogs.dir/640/files/2019/04/GSU-Archives-Camera-Use-Policy.pdf).**

### Agreement Regarding Confidential Information in Manuscripts

**We strive to identify personally identifiable information (PII) and confidential or restricted manuscripts before they are provided.** Due to collection size and processing levels, protected information may occasionally be overlooked.

**If you encounter protected information such as medical records, Social Security Numbers, or contact information:**

- **You must notify us immediately** if you discover personally identifiable or confidential information.
- **You may not disclose, distribute, or sell such information** once identified or informed of its confidentiality. Doing so may violate state or federal laws.
- **We may ask you to assure us that all confidential information has been removed** from your notes, databases, or other materials before you leave.
- **We may refuse duplication, transcription, or further access** **to materials** containing protected or confidential manuscripts.
