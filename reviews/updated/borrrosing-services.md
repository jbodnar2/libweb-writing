# Borrowing Services

## [Borrowing Services](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/)

Georgia State University students, faculty, staff, retirees, visiting researchers, and community borrowers who have current library accounts may borrow books and other eligible items from our collections. Borrowers are responsible for all items checked out on their account, including awareness of due dates. A courtesy notice is emailed before the loan is due and on the due date. Failure to receive or read a notice does not free the borrower of responsibilities.

#### [Intercampus Loan](https://answers.library.gsu.edu/faq/78930)

Request Georgia State books and physical media for pickup at Alpharetta, Atlanta, Clarkston, Decatur, Dunwoody, Newton, or Law Library.

#### [GIL Express](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/gil-express/)

Request books from University System of Georgia libraries.

#### [Interlibrary Loan (ILL)](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/interlibrary-loan/)

Request books, articles, and other materials that are **not** part of our collections.

#### [Desktop Delivery](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/desktop-delivery/)

Request electronic copies of articles and chapters from our print and microfilm collections.

#### [Storage Request](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/request-items-from-storage/)

Request DVDs, periodicals, and microfilm held in storage at the Atlanta campus.

#### [Borrowing Policies](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/borrowing-policies/)

Information on loan periods, renewals, and fines for students, faculty, staff, and other borrowers.

#### [Self Checkout](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/self-checkout/)

Fast, easy way to borrow books using your own device or from a kiosk near a service desk.

#### [Panther Pickup Lockers](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/panther-pickup-lockers/)

Convenient, contactless way to pick up requested materials at the Atlanta campus and skip the service desk.

#### [Renew Materials](/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/renew-materials/)

Information on how to renew materials.
