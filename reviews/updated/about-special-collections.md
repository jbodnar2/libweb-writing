# About Special Collections

We are a department of the University Library. Our mission is to collect and preserve unique historical materials that connect the Georgia State community, scholars and the public to the human experience.

[Our collections](/special-collections/collections/) are open to faculty, staff, and students, to outside scholars and to the public.

### Visit

We are located on the 8th floor of Library South (103 Decatur Street SE) in Atlanta.

- You must present a photo ID to enter the library and register to use our collections.
- You must make an appointment to view materials from our collections in the Reading Room.

[Make an Appointment](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon "Make an Appointment")

You may take photographs of our collections for personal use. Review our [camera use policy](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/tiv8g56pe2wultglm4z4u/GSU-Archives-Camera-Use-Policy.pdf?rlkey=ba4tg1uut72jhg68mczm45fk0&st=m2ml1n8c&dl=0) for more information about personal photography in the reading room and gallery.
