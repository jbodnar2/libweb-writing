# Tabling Request Form

## [Tabling Request Form](https://library.gsu.edu/about/policies/tabling-request-form/)

Georgia State University departments may request to reserve a space for tabling in the Altanta and Dunwoody campus University Library buildings.

Please note:

- We only accept requests from Georgia State University faculty and staff on behalf of departments.
- Requests must be placed at least 2 business days in advance of the requested time.
- We only accept requests from GSU departments.

**Clarkston campus tabling requests are not managed by the University Library**. To request tabling space at the Clarkston campus library building, please email [pcregreservation@gsu.edu](mailto:pcregreservation@gsu.edu).
