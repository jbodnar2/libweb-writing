### Instructional Visits

We invite you to request instruction sessions with archivists from Special Collections and Archives. We can introduce your students or members of your organization to archival research and our unique collections.

Fill out our [instruction request form](https://gsu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bBfXNyiOOaxjuGq) to arrange a visit. We require two weeks’ notice for instruction sessions and tours.

### Research Visits and Consultations

You are welcome to visit our Reading Room by appointment only to view our collections. You can use [our online registration portal](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon) to make an appointment and request materials to view. An archivist or other staff member will be available during your appointment to answer questions.

Remote research appointments are available on a limited basis. If you cannot physically travel to use our collections, please email [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu) and we will be happy to set up an appointment for remote research. Due to limited staffing and availability, these appointments are restricted to one 2 hour session or two 1 hour sessions per researcher. If your research requires more time, we ask that you please [schedule an appointment](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon) to do your research in person.

Do you have more in-depth research needs? Email us at [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu) to schedule a consultation with an archivist.

If you have questions about finding, using or citing archival material, see our [guide to archival research](http://research.library.gsu.edu/archivalresearch).

### Duplication Services

You may photograph or download most collection materials for personal use. For a fee, we provide photocopies and publication-quality reproductions. Please review our [pricing](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/7mlvcdr2sr0ztct73i37c/Reproduction_Price-List_2023.pdf?rlkey=6aes5yf7g0ggct0g3zvmz8393&st=up640fg9&dl=0) and [licensing](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/l7ixwrzpeb13fkwz1jrli/Licensing-Structure_2023.pdf?rlkey=db2nswo8njbqbm5l6q0gsrfbg&st=y2k33wqu&dl=0) guides for more information.

Using an item from our collection in a book, article, film, or other work may be subject to a licensing or usage fee. It may also require that you obtain separate permission from a copyright holder. If GSU does not hold the copyright, you must contact the copyright holder for permission. Identifying and contacting non-GSU rights holders is the responsibility of the researcher.

Contact us at [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu) if you have questions about our duplication services or copyright status and usage restrictions.

#### Restrictions

We will do our best to fulfill your request, but we might be unable to copy some items because of fragile condition, limited staff time, copyright issues or donor-imposed restrictions.

#### Duplication & Permission Requests

To request a copy of something from our collections or permission to publish it, submit a request through our [Aeon user portal](https://gsu.aeon.atlas-sys.com/logon).

If you’ve found these materials through the [Library Catalog](https://galileo-gsu.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?vid=01GALI_GSU:GSU_V1), [ArchivesSpace](https://archivesspace.library.gsu.edu/), or [Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.library.gsu.edu/), there will also be a _Request Item_ button that will lead you to the same Aeon portal to submit the request.

We will process your request as soon as we are able. Special Collections staff may reach out to you if they have any questions about your request.

#### Time to Fulfillment

Turnaround time for duplication services varies by request type and volume. Average times are as follows:

- Photocopy requests: one business day
- Scanning requests: three business days
- Audio and video duplication requests: 10 business days

Large-volume and complex orders may take longer.

### University Records Services

We acquire and preserve Georgia State’s permanently valuable records. University Archives services include locating relevant documents to support the university’s business needs and providing verified information about the university’s history. If you have questions about university records, contact [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu).

If you represent an administrative office that wants to transfer records to us, complete the following steps:

1.  Review “[Records Collected for University Archives](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/kze70p0h2vqi2f8ohnglm/UA_Records_Collected_Dec2018.pdf?rlkey=muck1cx0fttc0p80yqjwsvlew&st=syho0a5d&dl=0)” to decide which records to transfer.
2.  Complete the actions described in “[Procedures for Transfer of University Records to Archives](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/nm6jdmn590oiglpgk7pw5/UA_Transfer_Procedures_Feb2019.pdf?rlkey=0kvhytb97liiv4z66nfr5ag72&st=mxz7oxsw&dl=0).”
3.  Fill out the “[Request for Transfer of University Records to Archives](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/idmyzxkdescz1lxjmo96k/UA_Transfer_Form_Feb2019.pdf?rlkey=j71vnfelmbdjmh8rl9m7bjiv0&st=e9c7wls7&dl=0)” form and a [transfer inventory](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/sgue6hhssuavf1krfq0at/UA_Transfer_Inventory_Nov2018.xls?rlkey=m8dml5xarwxbm9tj0o91g7n8x&st=2s58pjnw&dl=0).
4.  Send the transfer form to the University Archives by interoffice mail or email to [archives@gsu.edu](mailto:archives@gsu.edu). The University Archivist will review and approve or deny your request.

Learn more about records management services and policies from the [GSU Office of the Registrar](https://registrar.gsu.edu/academic-records/records-management/) and the [USG Records Management Program](https://www.usg.edu/records_management/).
