# Storage Request

## [Storage Request](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/request-items-from-storage/)

Use this form to request materials held in storage at the Atlanta campus. Retrieval can take up to 2 hours. We will email you when your items are ready for pickup. Pick up requested materials at the service desk, Library North 1. Requested items are held for 10 days. See the [Borrowing Policies](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/borrowing-services/borrowing-policies/) for information on loan periods, renewals, and fines.

If you have questions, please call User Services at [404-413-2820](tel:404-413-2820).
