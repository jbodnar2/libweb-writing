# Research Data Services (RDS)

## [Research Data Services (RDS)](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/data-services/)

The **Research Data Services (RDS)** department at Georgia State University Library provides support to students, faculty, and staff in managing, analyzing, and visualizing research data. Whether you need help with data analysis tools, data visualization, or finding datasets, we’re here to assist you.

### What We Do

RDS offers a wide range of services to support your research needs:

- **Data Analysis Tools & Methods**: Assistance with statistical software like R, Python, SPSS, SAS, and Stata.
- **Data Visualization**: Help with tools like Tableau, Social Explorer, and ArcGIS.
- **Finding Data & Statistics**: Guidance on locating datasets and statistics for your research.
- **Data Collection**: Support for survey design, interview guides, and data collection methods.
- **Data Cleaning & Management**: Help with organizing, cleaning, and managing your data.
- **Generative AI & Data**: Resources and guidance on using AI tools responsibly in research.

### How We Help

We provide support through:

- [**Video Tutorials**](https://research.library.gsu.edu/rds/rds-workshops-recordings): On-demand training for data skills.
- **[Micro-Credential Badges](https://research.library.gsu.edu/rds/data-ready)**: Earn badges to showcase your data expertise.
- **Custom Sessions**: Tailored workshops for classes and research teams.
- **[One-on-One Help](https://research.library.gsu.edu/rds/quickdatahelp)**: Schedule appointments for personalized assistance.

Visit our [Research Data Services (RDS) guide](https://research.library.gsu.edu/rds) for more about our services.

### Scope of Services

RDS is here to **supplement** your research efforts, not replace them. We focus on troubleshooting specific software or methods needs and empowering you to do the work yourself. If your needs are beyond our scope, we’ll direct you to additional resources.

### Contact Us

Not sure who to contact? Email [libdatahelp@gsu.edu](mailto:libdatahelp@gsu.edu), and we’ll connect you with the right person.
