### Appointments & Consultations

Reference & Subject Librarians are available for virtual research consultations.

**Atlanta:** [Make a research appointment](https://rooms.library.gsu.edu/appointments) or use [our directory](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/research-services/librarians/) to find the appropriate subject librarian.

**Perimeter:**  Book a [research appointment](https://lib.gsu.edu/appt) with a Perimeter librarian.

### Walk-In

Service desk staff are available to answer questions and connect you with a librarian for more help. No appointment is necessary. See our [Visit the Library](https://library.gsu.edu/about/visit-the-library/) page for information about library location hours.

### Online Chat and Help & Answers

Librarians are ready to answer your questions online through our [Chat service and our Help & Answers knowledge base.](http://answers.library.gsu.edu/)

When chat is offline, you can search Help & Answers for help with frequently asked questions and common library issues.
