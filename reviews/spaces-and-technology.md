# Spaces & Technology

## [Spaces & Technology](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/)

Whether you need to borrow a computer or charger, are looking for a technology-rich space to meet with a group, or need to print a paper, the Library has you covered.

#### [Reserve a Space](/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/reserve-a-space/)

Reserve a variety of study or tech spaces.

#### [Borrow Equipment](/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/borrow-equipment/)

Reserve equipment in advance or check out first-come, first-serve equipment from the service desk.

#### [Computers & Software](/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/computers-and-software/)

Information about the Library’s computer lab areas and available software.

#### [CURVE](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/curve/)

The Library’s Collaborative University Research and Visualization Environment (CURVE) is a technology-rich discovery space supporting the research and digital scholarship of students, faculty, and staff.

#### [Print & Scan](/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/print-copy-and-scan/)

Details about printing and scanning services

#### [Study Commons](https://library.gsu.edu/services-and-spaces/spaces-and-technology/study-commons/)

**Open only to Georgia State students, faculty and staff**, the Study Commons is dedicated to focused learning and productive collaboration. This 19,000 square foot facility accommodates both individual and group study needs and offers extended hours to fit your busy schedule.
