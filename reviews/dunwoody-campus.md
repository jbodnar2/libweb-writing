# Dunwoody Campus Library

## [Dunwoody Campus Library](https://library.gsu.edu/about/visit-the-library/dunwoody-campus-library/)

The [Dunwoody Campus](https://perimeter.gsu.edu/student-experience/dunwoody-campus/) occupies 100 acres in north DeKalb County and serves as a community partner and institution of higher learning. Housed in Building NLRC, the Dunwoody Campus Library is one of Georgia State University’s six academic libraries. It provides a dynamic, inclusive environment that supports teaching, learning, and research for students, faculty, and staff.

### Services & Spaces

The library supports your academic work, whether you are researching, preparing for class, or collaborating with peers.

- **![Dunwoody Library](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2026/02/dunwoody-library.jpg)Resources**: Electronic and print collections, course reserves, and Open Educational Resources (OER).
- **Technology**: PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, laptops, Adobe Creative Cloud, graphing calculators, dry‑erase boards, makerspace (EXLab), and more.
- **Learning spaces**: Individual study desks, reservable group rooms, collaborative zones, and instructional areas.
- **Instruction**: Library workshops and sessions on research, professional skills, and information literacy.
- **Outreach**: Programs that connect library services with the broader campus community.
- **Support**: Staff assistance for research questions, resource use, and library instruction.
