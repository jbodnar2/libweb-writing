# Clarkston Campus Library

## [Clarkston Campus Library](https://library.gsu.edu/about/visit-the-library/clarkston-campus-library/)

The [Clarkston Campus](https://perimeter.gsu.edu/student-experience/clarkston-campus/) opened in 1964 as DeKalb College and has long been a cornerstone of the community. The **Jim Cherry Learning Resource Center**, also called the **Clarkston Campus Library**, is one of six academic libraries at Georgia State University. It offers an inclusive environment that supports teaching, learning, and research for students, faculty, and staff.

### Services and Spaces

The library supports your academic work, whether you are researching, preparing for class, or collaborating with peers.

- **![clarkston campus library entrance](https://library.gsu.edu/files/2026/02/clarkston-library.png)Resources**: Electronic and print collections, course reserves, and Open Educational Resources (OER).
- **Technology**: PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, laptops, Adobe Creative Cloud, graphing calculators, dry‑erase boards, and a rotating art gallery.
- **Learning spaces**: Individual study areas, group rooms, and collaborative zones.
- **Instruction**: Workshops and sessions on research methods, professional skills, and information literacy.
- **Outreach**: Programs that link library services with the wider community.
- **Information Hub (at the circulation desk)**:
  - Connects users to GSU Wi‑Fi.
  - Answers iCollege questions.
  - Checks out technology, cameras, and other media equipment from the [CATLAB](https://technology.gsu.edu/catlab/) (Monday‑Friday, 9 a.m.–6 p.m.; limited after 4 p.m.).
