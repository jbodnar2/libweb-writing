# Welcome to the GSU library.

The Georgia State University Library is the heart of the downtown campus. We are glad you are here to utilize our space. If you are lost, please click here to view a map of our facilities.

## Location & Hours of Operation

The main entrance to Library North is located at 103 Decatur St. S.E. in Atlanta. We serve a diverse population of students, including every freshman, sophomore, junior, and senior.

- **Monday - Thursday:** 7:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.
- **Friday:** 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 12:00 p.m. to 12:00 p.m.

Please note that the library closes at noon on Robinson College certain holidays. To read more about our holiday schedule, visit the gsu calendar.

## Services for Students and Faculty

We provide a wide array of resources for the underclassman and the seasoned researcher alike. Whether you are looking for books, journals, or digital media, our staff is here to help.

### 1. Technology & Printing

You can use your Panthercash to pay for printing, copying, and scanning. We have laptops available for 4-hour checkout; however, you must log-in to the portal to reserve one in advance.

### 2. Study Spaces

There are group study rooms located on Library North 2 and Library South 4. These rooms are perfect for working on projects, studying for exams, or meeting with classmates.

### 3. Special Collections and Archives

The Special Collections department is located on the 8th floor of Library South. It contains rare books, manuscripts, and university records. Please make sure to bring your Panther ID when you visit.

## Research & Instruction

Our librarians can help you find peer-reviewed articles, primary sources, and datasets. We offer workshops throughout the semester to help you master the research process. If you want to see the full list of workshops, please check our website at www.library.gsu.edu.
