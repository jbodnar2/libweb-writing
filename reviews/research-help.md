# Research Help

## [Research Help](https://library.gsu.edu/research/research-help/)

Starting a research project can be tricky, but the library has resources and people to get you on the right path.

### [Find Books & Articles](/research/research-help/find-books-articles/)

Books can help you answer those big research questions, like “What were the major causes of the Cold War?” or “How have African-Americans been depicted throughout film history?” Articles help fill in details and provide in-depth analysis on specialized topics. This page explains how to find both.

### [Talk with a Librarian](/research/research-help/talk-with-a-librarian/)

Not sure how to get started? Got a super-complicated question? Librarians help you focus your research and find materials to support your learning.

### [Check our Research Guides](http://research.library.gsu.edu/?b=s)

These guides contain our librarians’ top tips on resources and other tools relevant to your area of study.

### [Citing & Bibliographies](http://research.library.gsu.edu/sb.php?subject_id=26073)

APA! MLA! Chicago! Citation can be confusing. Here are some resources to get you on the right track.
