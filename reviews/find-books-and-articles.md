#### How to Find Articles

1.  Locate the relevant database by browsing the library’s [databases list](/databases/) or checking the [research guide](http://research.library.gsu.edu/) for your subject or class.
2.  Select a database to search. If you’re off-campus, you’ll be asked to enter your campus ID and password.
3.  Enter your search terms and browse your results.
4.  If you aren’t getting the results you want, [talk with a librarian](/research/research-help/talk-with-a-librarian/) who can help you formulate more effective search strategies.

NOTE: Some databases include article text or PDF. Some don’t. To find the complete article, use the *Find it @GSU* button or search the [library catalog](http://gilfind.gsu.edu/) for the journal title (not the article title).

If we don’t have the article you need, you can request it via our [Interlibrary Loan](https://gsu.illiad.oclc.org/illiad/lending/lendinglogon.html) (ILL) service.
