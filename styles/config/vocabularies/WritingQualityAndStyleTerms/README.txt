PURPOSE:

This vocabulary targets general prose quality and web accessibility.

ACCEPT LIST:
Used to whitelist specific industry-standard idioms or phrases that otherwise trigger "cliché" or "wordiness" rules but are required for our specific library context.

REJECT LIST:

Identifies "lazy" or overly formal web-writing terms (e.g., utilize, subsequent, assistance) that should be replaced with simpler, more active alternatives (e.g., use, later, help) to improve readability for students in a hurry.
