PURPOSE:

This vocabulary manages names of donors, scholars, and staff.

ACCEPT LIST:

Validates the spelling of names mentioned in collection descriptions or staff directories (e.g., Johnny Mercer, Eudora Welty) so they do not trigger spelling or complexity alerts.

REJECT LIST:

Used to identify and flag misspelled names of prominent figures or former staff members to prevent historically inaccurate or disrespectful typos in permanent records.
