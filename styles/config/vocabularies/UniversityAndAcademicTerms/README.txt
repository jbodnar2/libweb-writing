PURPOSE:

This vocabulary manages general academic and university-specific terminology.

ACCEPT LIST:

Includes necessary high-syllable academic words (e.g., interdisciplinary, undergraduate, pedagogy) that are essential to the university mission and should not be flagged as "complex."

REJECT LIST:

Identifies "bureaucratic" or formal administrative terms (e.g., patron, dissemination, consolidation) that should be replaced with student-friendly alternatives like "user," "share," or "merger."
